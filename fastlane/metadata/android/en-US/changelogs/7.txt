- Installing a new version no longer needs deleting the old one (releases now
  use one signing key).
- Fixed invisible text in the studio and the library.
- Cleaner pitch pad and a colour snap chip; the bookmark button toggles.
- Reliable loop creation, fuller loop bars, and scrubbing that follows your
  finger and commits on release.
- The render sheet picks the format inline, defaults loop-only off, and has a
  confirmed cancel. Renders run in the background and notify you when saved.
- A pitch name and a file name are now separate; tapping a pitch opens the
  render sheet and long-press gives render, rename, share, and delete.
- Shorter onboarding, with the speed HUD shown on the speed step.
