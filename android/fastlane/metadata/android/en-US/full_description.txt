pitcher transposes audio by any pitch interval while keeping the tempo, and lets
you keep a shelf of pitch variants per track.

It is fully offline. The app requests no network permission and bundles no
downloader. Import audio (or video, from which only the audio is kept) from your
device, or share a file into pitcher from any other app.

The interface is built for touch. A short guided tour runs the first time you
open a song and points out the gestures and controls; you can replay it from
Settings at any time.

Features:
- Transpose by any amount in cents or semitones, with live preview: pitch moves
  while tempo stays fixed.
- A full-screen pitch gesture: drag up or down to shift, and hold left of where
  you touched for coarse steps or right for 1-cent fine control. Snap to the
  nearest semitone, or type an exact value.
- A waveform you can pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and tap to seek, with time
  stamps and bookmark snapping.
- Drag the lane above the waveform to set an A/B loop, or use the loop chip.
- Change the play speed with a hold-and-slide preset picker from 0.5x to 2x.
- Detect the note at the playhead with a monophonic tuner, or type a note or a
  frequency. Target note and target Hz fill each other in.
- Keep every pitch you like; the shelf lists the original plus each variant,
  sorted by how far it shifted. Switching pitches never moves the playhead.
  Long-press a saved pitch to rename, export, share, or delete it.
- Bookmarks on the waveform.
- Export and share a pitch as WAV, MP3, M4A, or Opus, optionally only the A/B
  loop. Renders can be cancelled, and exports keep stereo where the device
  allows.
- Screen stays on while you work, and playback continues in the background.

pitcher is free and open source software (GPL-3.0-or-later). No ads, no
tracking, no accounts.
