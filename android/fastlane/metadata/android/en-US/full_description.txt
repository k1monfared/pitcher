pitcher transposes audio by any pitch interval while keeping the tempo, and lets
you keep a shelf of pitch variants per track.

It is fully offline. The app requests no network permission and bundles no
downloader. Import audio (or video, from which only the audio is kept) from your
device, or share a file into pitcher from any other app.

The interface is built for touch. A short guided tour runs the first time you
open a song and points out the gestures and controls; you can replay it from
Settings at any time.

Features:
- Transpose by any amount in cents or semitones, with live preview: pitch moves
  while tempo stays fixed.
- A full-screen pitch gesture: drag up or down to shift. The rate follows how
  fast you move, so slow is fine and fast is coarse, and a flick keeps going and
  slows down until you catch it. Snap to a 20-cent grid with a stronger pull to
  semitones.
- A timeline: make multiple loops by dragging the lane above the wave, and add
  bookmarks. Long-press a loop edge or a bookmark pin to fine-tune it. Pinch to
  zoom, drag to pan, long-press to scrub, tap to seek.
- A repeat button cycles whole song, all loops, or one loop. Loops can be turned
  off individually, and renders join the enabled loops into one file.
- A tuner: keep a source and a target note, detected at the playhead or a
  bookmark or typed, and apply the interval to the slider in one tap.
- Detect the note at the playhead, or type a note or a frequency.
- Keep every pitch you like; the shelf lists the original plus each variant.
  Long-press a saved pitch to rename, share, or delete it.
- One render sheet: choose the folder, edit the file name, pick a format, and
  Save file or Share. Renders can be cancelled.
- Export and share as WAV, MP3, M4A, or Opus, with stereo where the device
  allows.
- Screen stays on while you work, and playback continues in the background.

pitcher is free and open source software (GPL-3.0-or-later). No ads, no
tracking, no accounts.
