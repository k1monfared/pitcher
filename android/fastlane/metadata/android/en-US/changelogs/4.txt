- New touch-first Studio interface with a full-screen pitch gesture: drag up or
  down to shift, slide left or right of your touch to change the speed of the
  change, from coarse to fine.
- Giant live cents readout, a HUD with a semitone scale, and haptics.
- SoundCloud-style waveform with draggable A/B loop handles.
- Per-song pitch carousel, and a speed chip you drag or tap.
- Render and export are separate: renders save to Music/pitcher and stay in the
  library.
- Switch between the new and classic interfaces in Settings.
